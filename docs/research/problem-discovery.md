# Problem discovery and validation plan

## Current problem hypothesis

People who want to learn, creators who want to package knowledge, and affiliates who want to promote useful products may need to move between disconnected tools. Enski explores whether a connected platform can make these journeys simpler.

This is a hypothesis—not a verified finding. No interview sample, survey result or market statistic is claimed in this document.

## Assumptions to test

1. Learners value one place to discover courses, digital resources and related opportunities.
2. Creators value a simpler workflow for presenting and managing knowledge products.
3. Affiliates need transparent campaign terms, tracking and earnings information.
4. Role-specific workspaces reduce confusion compared with a single generic dashboard.

## Research plan

### Interviews
Speak with learners, independent creators and people who promote digital products. Ask about recent behaviour and actual workarounds rather than pitching Enski.

### Usability testing
Give participants realistic tasks:
- Find a resource for a stated learning goal.
- Compare two product detail pages.
- Prepare a digital product for publication.
- Find the terms and performance of an affiliate promotion.

Observe completion, hesitation, errors, questions and workarounds.

### Synthesis
For each finding, capture:
- Participant context (anonymised).
- Observed behaviour or verbatim note with consent.
- User problem.
- Frequency and severity.
- Confidence and evidence.
- Product opportunity.
- Follow-up question.

## Research ethics

Obtain informed consent, avoid collecting unnecessary personal information, anonymise notes and do not publish identifiable participant data.
