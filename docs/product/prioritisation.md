# Prioritisation approach

## Method

Use a lightweight RICE-style assessment when there is enough evidence to estimate reach, impact, confidence and effort. When evidence is weak, record assumptions rather than manufacturing precise scores.

For urgent, high-risk or compliance-related work, use risk and dependency assessment alongside impact.

## Suggested priority order for product readiness

### P0 — Trust and foundations
- Replace demo-only authentication before real users or private data are introduced.
- Enforce role permissions at the backend.
- Define payment, wallet and payout contracts before enabling real money movement.
- Protect secrets and validate all trusted operations server-side.
- Make prototype and production states unmistakable.

### P1 — Core user journeys
- Learner discovery and resource detail.
- Creator product creation and management.
- Affiliate promotion and performance visibility.
- Reliable empty, loading, success and error states.

### P2 — Growth and optimisation
- Improve search and filtering based on observed usage.
- Improve creator storefront customisation.
- Add lifecycle messaging and product analytics where consent and privacy requirements are met.
- Run usability tests and prioritise observed friction.

## Scoring discipline

For every candidate item, record:
- User group affected.
- Evidence of the problem.
- Reach estimate and source.
- Expected impact and rationale.
- Confidence level.
- Effort estimate and dependencies.
- Risk if delayed.

Do not label a roadmap item as committed until ownership, scope and dependencies are agreed.
