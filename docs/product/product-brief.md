# Product brief

## Summary

Enski is a digital learning and creator-platform concept that brings learning-resource discovery, creator products and promotional opportunities into a connected experience.

## Vision

Help people learn valuable skills, access relevant opportunities and share or monetise their knowledge through a coherent digital platform.

## Product opportunity

The product explores the opportunity to reduce fragmentation between finding learning resources, accessing support such as coaching or events, and discovering creator-led products.

This opportunity is a hypothesis to validate. The repository does not contain evidence of completed market sizing or formal user research sufficient to claim product-market fit.

## Product principles

- Make the next useful action obvious.
- Design around user goals, not internal team structure.
- Keep role-specific experiences focused.
- Make money, access and status information understandable.
- Build trust through clear feedback and predictable interactions.
- Validate assumptions before treating them as facts.

## Primary audiences

- Learners looking for courses, resources, coaching and events.
- Creators who want to present and manage knowledge products.
- Affiliates who participate in product promotion.
- Operational users who support and manage the platform.

## Success measures to validate

Potential measures—not current performance claims:
- Discovery-to-product-detail conversion.
- Product-detail-to-enrolment or purchase conversion.
- Learner activation and repeat learning activity.
- Creator product publication completion rate.
- Affiliate activation and attributable conversions.
- Support request rate and time to resolution.

Metrics need event definitions, instrumentation and baseline data before targets are set.
