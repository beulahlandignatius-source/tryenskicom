# User groups and proto-personas

These are **proto-personas** derived from the product's role structure and feature areas. They are working assumptions, not research-validated personas. Validate them through interviews, behavioural data and usability testing.

## Learner

**Goal:** Find useful learning resources and make progress.

**Needs:** Relevant discovery, clear product details, transparent pricing and access information, a dependable learning journey.

**Potential friction:** Unclear course value, fragmented discovery, uncertainty about what happens after enrolment or purchase.

## Creator

**Goal:** Package knowledge into products and reach an audience.

**Needs:** Straightforward product setup, a credible storefront, clear product status, understandable sales and payout information.

**Potential friction:** Complex publishing steps, uncertainty about product visibility, unclear fees or payout timing.

## Affiliate

**Goal:** Promote suitable products and understand the results.

**Needs:** Clear campaign terms, reliable promotional links, understandable attribution and earnings information.

**Potential friction:** Ambiguous commission rules, missing tracking information, unclear payout status.

## Admin or support operator

**Goal:** Resolve user and platform issues while protecting account and transaction integrity.

**Needs:** Role-appropriate access, searchable records, clear status history and auditable actions.

**Potential friction:** Incomplete records, excessive manual work, unclear permission boundaries.

## Validation questions

- What task brings each group to Enski most often?
- What do they currently use instead?
- What information do they need before trusting a transaction?
- Where do they abandon a workflow?
- Which tasks are frequent, urgent or high-risk?
